# The quality bar

Psyrend is judged by someone watching a whole song, then a whole playlist. This page collects every requirement the user has stated, with a measurable check wherever one exists. A visual that fails any of these does not ship in auto-direction.

## What the user asked for

1. **Generated by the music.** Every motion on screen has a musical cause you can feel: a kick lands, a melody note rises, a chord changes, a section arrives. Nothing "does its own thing". Free-running rotation, drift or swirling that ignores the music is a defect.
2. **Not a MIDI display.** The analysis is the input, not the picture. Don't draw notes, piano rolls or spectrograms. The music *shapes* a world.
3. **Never repetitive.** In a 30-minute session (about eight songs) you should rarely see a recognisable visual twice. A recolour or re-ripple of the same core counts as the same visual. **No world appears twice in one song**, not even for a returning chorus.
4. **A journey with a story.** A song is a ride through places that follow its form: builds tense up, drops arrive, breakdowns breathe. The places are not shuffled presets.
5. **Immersive and abstract.** You are inside a world that fills the frame with depth, not looking at an object in a void. Nothing is recognizable: no stages, planets, eyes, temples, gems, forests, landscapes or cities.
6. **Refined and detailed.** Nothing chunky, plain or basic. No plastic tubes, slabs or primitive shapes. Detail comes from structure (fractals, fine filaments, light), not from noise.
7. **Clean.** One idea per place. Post effects (trails, kaleidoscope, aberration) are used deliberately, never as a crutch, and there are no decorative textures.
8. **Constant, fluid motion, never static.** Structures are always evolving into new shapes (the morph engine, `src/scenes/morph.ts`), even in quiet passages, and the camera glides at a steady pace. Motion eases down only after a long quiet stretch. Beats never jolt motion: no surges, lunges, swells or zoom punches. No shake and no jitter.
9. **A beat permanently changes the structure.** Nothing pulses out and back: a kick or snare moves the form, its colour or its detail to a new, lasting state, eased in over a fraction of a beat (`morph.at` steps, `kickPhase`/`snarePhase`/`notePhase`). The change travels: a front sweeps through the structure and leaves it changed behind it. Each event looks different. A whole-screen flash is not a reaction.
10. **Clearance.** The camera never collides with or skims surfaces; nothing blurry fills the lens.
11. **Full resolution.** Hold 60 fps or more. Lower the resolution only near 30 fps.

## Measured checks

`node scripts/audit.mjs react --file <wav> --from <t> --dur 6 [--pin <scene>]` renders frames offline and reports the **lift**: how much the picture changes in the 100 ms after each kind of event, compared with the 200 ms before it. A lift of 1.0 means the picture ignores that event.

| Check | Bar | How to measure |
|---|---|---|
| Kick | lift ≥ 2.0 in an energetic stretch | `react` on Bass Face 60–66 s or the demo at 50 s |
| Snare | lift ≥ 1.5 | same |
| Melody | lead lift ≥ 1.5 | same; also Doorframe 40–46 s |
| Self-motion | `corr` ≥ 0.3 (change follows the onsets) | same |
| Repetition | no world twice in a song; none twice in a three-song session | `audit.mjs variety --files a,b,c` |
| Cost | ≤ 2 ms GPU at 720p | `verify.mjs` gpu= |

Scenes and layers that fail the bar are excluded from auto-direction with `auto: false` (scenes) or `weight: 0` (layers). They can still be pinned or forced by hand.

`node scripts/audit.mjs batch --file <wav> --from 60 --dur 6` measures every scene and layer at once; summarise the result with `node scripts/audit-table.mjs <jsonl>`.

## Music inputs every visual can read

- **Rhythm:** `F.kick`, `F.snare`, `F.hat`, `F.onset` (envelopes); `F.kickNext` (anticipation); `F.beatPhase`, `F.barPhase`, `F.beatPulse`, `F.barPulse`; `F.bpm`.
- **Melody:** `F.lead` (attack envelope), `F.leadPitch` (−1..1, gliding), `F.leadHeld`. Sculpt layers also get the gesture (`gestAt`) and the notes buffer.
- **Harmony:** `F.chordHue`, `F.chordPulse`, `F.tension`, `F.majorness`, `F.keyHue`, and the palette `F.pal0..3`.
- **Form and energy:** `F.energy`, `F.energySlow`, `F.sectionProgress`, `F.anticipation`, `F.dropPulse`, `F.intensity`.
- **Spectrum:** `F.sub`, `F.bass`, `F.lowMid`, `F.mid`, `F.high`, `F.centroid`, `F.spectrum`, `F.chroma`.

Time-based motion (travel, rotation) should advance with the beat, so it moves on the music and rests in silence, and with energy, so it is faster when the song is.
